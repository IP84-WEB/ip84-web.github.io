Iacopo Pasqui

Iacopo Pasqui was born in Florence in 1984.
He is a photographer specialising in Fine Art and documentary photography.
He has won numerous awards and competitions, including:
Strategia Fotografia 2023 and Italian Council 2023, both promoted by MiC;
Young Italian Photography #07 at the European Photography Festival in 2019.
He has been a member of Future Talents Photographers since 2019.
He won the Leica Talent 24x36 and the competition Panorami Contemporanei e Luoghi in Trasformazione – Residenze di Fotografia in Italia, promoted by MiBACT and GAI (2017).
He is among the photographers included in the volume Storia della fotografia in Italia. Dal 1839 ad oggi by G.D'Autilia (Einaudi, Turin 2021).
His works are part of the collection of the Fondazione Musei Civici di Reggio Emilia.

He has published and collaborated with public and private institutions, such as:
ICCD, MiC, IIC Mexico City, IIC London, IIC Stockholm, Istituto della Enciclopedia Italiana G.Treccani, Fondazione Musei Civici di Reggio Emilia, GAI and others.
He has published for publishing houses, newspapers and magazines such as:
Einaudi, Witty Books, L’Espresso, Financial Times, Frankfurter Allgemeine Quarterly, Der Zeit Magazine, Atmos, Volks, Volkstrant Magazine,
Internazionale, D di Repubblica and others. It is represented by the German Connected Archive based in Berlin.
